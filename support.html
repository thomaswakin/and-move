And Move, by The Simple Group

And Move is designed to be simple, so there isn't much to learn — but here are quick answers to common questions. If you need anything else, just email us.

How do I check in?

Tap the big box on the Today screen. A list of activities appears — pick whatever you did. Nothing is recorded until you choose, and you can pick more than one.

What counts as moving?

Whatever you say counts. A walk, the gym, pilates, a dance class, a swim — if you consider it exercise, check it in. You can add your own activities with + Add, or from Settings.

What is a rest day?

A deliberate day off. Pick Rest in the activity list and the day is still logged — And Move counts intentional check-ins, not just activity, so a rest day keeps your streak alive rather than breaking it.

How does the streak work?

Days straight counts every day you logged, whether you moved or rested. A missed day ends it. In Settings → Allow rest days in a row you choose how many rest days can sit back to back before the streak resets — set it to 0 if you want any rest day to reset it.

What's the difference between "days active" and the streak?

Days active is how many days you actually moved, all time — rest days aren't counted. The streak stats are about showing up; this one is about movement. Tap any number to see the breakdown.

I forgot to check in yesterday. Can I fix it?

Yes. Tap any past day in the week strip at the bottom of Today, or any day in the Calendar tab, and fill it in. You can't fill in future days.

Can I add notes to an activity?

Tap an activity's pill after you've checked in and a notes box opens for that activity on that day. You can type, or tap the microphone and speak — and you can hit Save while the microphone is still running.

Is there a widget?

Yes. Add the And Move widget to your Home Screen to check in without opening the app. The medium size also lets you tap specific activities and mark a rest day.

How do reminders work?

Turn on Settings → Daily reminder and pick a time. It's skipped automatically on days you've already checked in, and you can log the day straight from the notification.

Does it sync across my devices?

Yes. With iCloud enabled, your history syncs privately through your own iCloud account. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud and that iCloud Drive is on.
