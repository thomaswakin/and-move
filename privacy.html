Privacy Policy

And Move, by The Simple Group · Effective October 10, 2026

And Move is built to respect your privacy. In short: we don't collect your data, we can't see your activity, and there is no tracking or advertising.

Information we collect

None. And Move has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. The Simple Group does not collect, store, or receive any personal information about you.

Your activity and content

The days you check in, the activities you record, your rest days, and any notes you write stay on your device. If you have iCloud enabled, they also sync privately through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so your history appears on your other devices. This data lives in your personal iCloud — The Simple Group has no access to it and never receives a copy. You can remove any entry in the app at any time, or remove all data by deleting the app and clearing its iCloud data in iOS Settings.

Microphone and speech

Writing notes by voice is optional. When you use it, And Move uses your microphone and Apple's Speech Recognition solely to turn what you say into note text. Depending on your device and settings, Apple may process that audio on your device or send it to Apple's servers for transcription, as described in Apple's Privacy Policy. Either way it is never sent to, stored by, or seen by The Simple Group. The microphone is used only while you are actively dictating, and you can turn it off at any time.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is scheduled locally on your device. No reminder data leaves your phone, and The Simple Group is not notified whether you check in.

Health data

And Move does not read from or write to Apple Health, and does not access your steps, workouts, or any other health or fitness data.

Third parties

And Move contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and The Simple Group operates no servers that receive your data. The only data processing involved is Apple's own iCloud sync and speech recognition, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

And Move does not collect data from anyone, including children, and is safe for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
